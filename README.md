# Nykaa Beauty & Fashion Analytics

An end-to-end **SQL and Power BI analytics project** focused on analyzing Nykaa's Beauty & Fashion business data to generate actionable business insights.

This project combines **SQL-based data analysis** with an interactive **Power BI dashboard** to analyze sales performance, product categories, customers, orders, inventory, and operational performance.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance
- Compare Beauty and Fashion business performance
- Identify top-performing products, brands, and categories
- Analyze customer value and membership segments
- Monitor order and shipping status
- Evaluate inventory levels and stock availability
- Identify low-stock products and inventory risks
- Track monthly business and operational trends
- Build interactive dashboards for business decision-making

## Tools & Technologies

- **SQL** – Data analysis, queries, joins, subqueries, CTEs, window functions, stored procedures and triggers
- **Power BI** – Data visualization, DAX measures, KPIs and interactive dashboards
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data handling and supporting analysis

## Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard contains three main analytical sections:

1. **Executive Insights** – Overall revenue, orders, customers, AOV, growth and category performance
2. **Category Merchandising** – Beauty vs Fashion performance, brands, categories, membership tiers and order status
3. **Inventory & Operations** – Stock levels, inventory value, low-stock SKUs, order status, stock availability and operational trends

The dashboard is designed with interactive filters and navigation to provide a clear view of business performance.
